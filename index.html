chaos

Marker drawings by shotapop

I started this little project because I was too focused on making everything perfect. I hyperanalyzed every line I made, but my hand tremors wouldn't let me draw a straight one. So I made these pages to prove to myself that nothing is supposed to be perfect. There is art in chaos and imperfection.

I never used black. I didn't want it to interfere with the color. There was no plan for any page: I followed the lines and let them lead, the way a zen drawing practice works, and this is where they went.
